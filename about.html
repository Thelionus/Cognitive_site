The Pedigree

Born from Deep Research

The Cognitive Group, headquartered in Montreal, is a specialized spinoff of the Computer Research Institute of Montreal (CRIM).

Leveraging decades of proprietary cognitive analysis capturing expert reasoning not present in public datasets, we partnered with the Royal Bank of Canada for over a decade to optimize high-stakes loan origination.
