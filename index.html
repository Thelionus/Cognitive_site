The Method

Four Steps From Expert Judgment to a Decision

No single model is trusted on its own. Every recommendation passes through the same four steps, and the final decision always belongs to your people.

01

Expert observation

We sit with your senior analysts while they work real files and record how they reason: what they check, in what order, and what makes them stop. That becomes the agent's playbook.

02

Two models check each other

At least two agents running on models from different vendors process the same file. Each must return a strict JSON output against the same schema, and the outputs are compared field by field. Any disagreement is flagged, never averaged away.

03

AI supervisor

A supervising agent reviews the whole file and the flagged disagreements, and raises anything that needs a human's attention. It is live today at Al Rahma in Senegal.

Al Rahma case study →
04

The human decides

Your analyst sees the recommendation, the evidence behind it and every flag, then approves, changes or rejects it. That decision is recorded in BusinessBANKER.
