Security

Security at Cognitive Group starts with the same principle behind our product: nothing should operate as a black box. Every architectural and operational control below exists to keep decisions auditable, data isolated, and access traceable — for both our corporate systems and the platform we deploy inside client environments.

Glass Box Architecture

Cognitive Core's lending and underwriting decisions are made by a deterministic rules engine, not a generative model — every decision produces a fully traceable audit log showing exactly which rule, threshold, and data point drove the outcome. AI (via AI Workbench) is scoped strictly to document and data capture, never to the final decision itself.

Data Isolation

Each client deployment is logically isolated. No underwriting data, model output, or decision logic from one institution is ever visible to, or used to train systems for, another client.

Multi-Model Cross-Validation

Where AI Workbench uses large language models (e.g. Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, Mistral) for document and data extraction, outputs are cross-checked against each other. Disagreements are automatically escalated to a human reviewer rather than silently resolved — reducing the risk of a single model's hallucination reaching a decision.

Access Controls & Monitoring

  • Role-based access control for every user of the platform, aligned to each institution's existing entitlement model.
  • Full audit logging of configuration changes to deployed decision workflows.
  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest.

Regulatory Alignment

Cognitive's deterministic, fully-auditable decision trail is designed to support institutions' own compliance obligations under frameworks such as Basel IV and OSFI guidance. Cognitive does not itself certify an institution's regulatory compliance — each client's compliance and risk teams remain responsible for validating a deployment against their specific regulatory requirements.

Responsible Disclosure

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability affecting our website or platform, please report it to security@cognitivegroup.com. We ask researchers to give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.

Contact

For security documentation requests as part of a vendor risk assessment, contact Cognitive Group at security@cognitivegroup.com.
